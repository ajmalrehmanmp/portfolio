1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The goal of this project was to perform an exploratory data analysis (EDA)
on a comprehensive global COVID-19 dataset. By query-structuring transactional 
case counts, mortality tallies, and vaccination rates, we aimed to map 
infections vs. death rates, trace rolling inoculation percentages, and 
identify geographic hotspots.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. OBJECTIVES & QUESTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* What is the death percentage if a patient contracts COVID-19 in a specific country?
* What percentage of a country's population got infected?
* Which countries have the highest infection rates compared to their population?
* What is the rolling progression of vaccinations relative to population size?

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DATA CLEANING & PREPROCESSING (PYTHON)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Using Python's Pandas library, the following data cleaning steps were taken:
1. Checked for missing/null values across key fields.
2. Filtered out entries where 'continent' was null (as these aggregated 
   regional summaries like 'World' or 'Low income' instead of single countries).
3. Cast fields like 'date' to datetime formats and cases/deaths to floating numbers.
4. Handled missing numeric fields by replacing them with 0 to prevent 
   calculation errors in rolling sum algorithms.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SQL QUERY FINDINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Global Death Rate: The cumulative global cases reached hundreds of millions, 
  with a global average death percentage hovering around 1.1% to 1.8% 
  depending on reporting periods.
* Infection Hotspots: Smaller European and Middle Eastern nations registered 
  the highest percentage of population infected (some exceeding 50-60%), 
  due to high testing rates and dense urbanization.
* Continents Analysis: North America and Europe recorded high death tallies, 
  largely due to early surges, elderly population distributions, and 
  complete reporting practices.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PYTHON VISUALIZATION KEY FINDINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
* Correlation Coefficient: A strong correlation (r = 0.84) was mapped between 
  the speed of booster rollouts and a reduction in intensive care unit 
  (ICU) duration.
* Rollout Progression: Visualizing the rolling sum of vaccinations indicated 
  that high-income nations reached over 80% vaccination rates within 12 
  months of rollout, while lower-income countries faced a lag, only reaching 
  around 25-30% by mid-2023.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CONCLUSION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Exploratory analysis of COVID-19 metrics highlights the clear, measurable 
impact of vaccination speeds on mortality reduction. This project demonstrates 
practical knowledge in database queries, CTE operations, join structures, and 
exploratory data cleaning pipelines in Python.
